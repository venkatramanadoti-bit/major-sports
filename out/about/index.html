About MAJOR SPORTS

Your trusted local sports gear hub in Medchal, Secunderabad. Since 2020, we\'ve been serving athletes with quality equipment, expert advice, and a passion for sports.

Connect on WhatsAppVisit Our Store

Our Mission

To empower every athlete in Medchal and beyond with access to authentic, high-quality sports equipment at fair prices, backed by expert guidance and genuine care for their sporting journey.

We believe that the right equipment can transform your game, prevent injuries, and enhance enjoyment. Whether you\'re a beginner picking up your first racket or a seasoned competitor chasing championships, we\'re here to help you find your perfect match.

Commitment to Authenticity

Every product is sourced from authorized distributors

Our Values

These principles guide everything we do - from product selection to customer service.

Passion for Sports

We live and breathe sports. Every product we carry is selected by athletes who understand what you need to perform your best.

Quality Assurance

We partner only with trusted brands and verify every product. Your safety and satisfaction are our top priorities.

Community First

We're not just a store - we're part of the Medchal sports community. Supporting local athletes is in our DNA.

Expert Guidance

Our team includes former players and coaches who can help you choose the right gear for your level and goals.

Competitive Pricing

We believe quality sports equipment should be accessible. Fair prices without compromising on authenticity.

Continuous Improvement

We constantly update our inventory with the latest innovations and listen to customer feedback to serve you better.

Our Journey

From a small local shop to Medchal\'s premier sports destination.

  2. 2020

    Founded in Medchal

    Started as a small local sports shop with a big vision: to bring quality sports equipment to Medchal athletes.

  3. 2021

    Expanded Product Range

    Added badminton, cricket, football, and fitness categories. Became the go-to store for multiple sports.

  4. 2022

    Community Partnerships

    Partnered with local schools, clubs, and tournaments. Started sponsoring youth sports programs.

  5. 2023

    Digital Presence Launched

    Launched website and WhatsApp ordering. Made it easier for customers to browse and enquire remotely.

  6. 2024

    Recognized Excellence

    Awarded "Best Sports Retailer - Medchal" by local business association. 5-star customer rating achieved.

  7. 2025

    Continued Growth

    Expanding inventory, improving services, and deepening community connections. The journey continues.
