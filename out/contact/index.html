Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. Can't find what you're looking for? WhatsApp us!

What are your store hours?
We're open Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 9:00 PM, and Sunday 11:00 AM - 7:00 PM. Hours may vary on holidays.
Do you offer online ordering and delivery?
Currently we offer WhatsApp-based ordering with in-store pickup. We're working on adding delivery options soon.
Can I check product availability before visiting?
Yes! Use the WhatsApp button on any product page or contact us directly. We'll check real-time stock for you.
Do you provide racket stringing services?
Yes, we offer professional racket stringing for badminton and tennis rackets with various string options.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, UPI, credit/debit cards, and bank transfers. WhatsApp orders can be paid on pickup.
Do you have warranty on products?
All products carry manufacturer warranties. We're an authorized dealer for all brands we carry.
Can I return or exchange products?
Returns/exchanges within 7 days with original packaging and receipt. Conditions apply - check with staff.
Do you sponsor local teams or events?
Yes! We actively support local sports clubs, school teams, and tournaments. Contact us for sponsorship enquiries.
